Why This Proposal

Current video editors can be prompted to alter scenes, but physical correction is more demanding: the model must preserve the original scene while identifying or following a physically grounded repair target. The demos separate explicit repair, blind self-correction, and no-op preservation under hallucinated feedback.

Sample Feedback Signals

  • GT feedback: water-contact and cup-rim failures from Physion-Eval.
  • GPT-5.5 feedback: mug handle/body consistency detected from frames.
  • Self correction: generic instruction to repair physically unrealistic behavior.
  • No-issue robustness: realistic kitchen input should remain stable even when feedback is noisy or absent.
